3 Thoughtful Conversation Prompts for Giulio (For Next Lesson)
Prompt 01 • The 16-Second Elevator Pitch

Concise Persuasion Under Pressure

Pick a workplace proposal, audio tech upgrade, or public policy idea. Set a 16-second timer and pitch your argument directly, anchoring your points around tangible citizen impact (quality of life), concrete actions, and measurable outcomes.

Prompt 02 • Conceptual Nuance

Distinguishing Near-Synonyms

Practice explaining the delicate boundary between two related philosophical or emotional states without using their direct names (e.g., Knowledge vs. Wisdom, Ambition vs. Contentment, or Love vs. Attachment). Highlight who benefits and where superficiality ends.

Prompt 03 • Storytelling with Suspense

The Strategic Narrative Twist

Recount a surprising real-life experience (such as your Mallorca wedding encounter or meeting FC Barcelona staff). Structure your narrative so that the surprising identity or unexpected outcome is withheld until the very last sentence to build maximum curiosity.
