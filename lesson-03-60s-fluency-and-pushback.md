## 2. 🔧 5 Quick Polishes (From Nov 9)

| What came out last week | Natural Native English | Quick Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Think **to** an outsider / think **to** Peele"* | *"Think **of** an outsider / think **about** Peele"* | Always **`think of`** or **`think about`** (not *to*). |
| *"Writing **on the socials**"* | *"Posting **on social media**"* | **`Social media`** is uncountable (no *the*, no *-s*). |
| *"Have a dinner **on** the same table"* | *"Have dinner **at** the same table"* | Sit **`at`** a table; **`have dinner`** (no *a*). |
| *"A desire **of him**"* | *"A desire **of his**"* / *"Whether **he wants to**"* | Use **`of his / of mine / of theirs`**. |
| *"Something that **you could trust**"* | *"Something **believable** / **convincing**"* | Stories and characters are **`believable`**. |

## 3. ⚙️ Part 2: The 60-Second Executive Frame (Work & Client Calls)

The exact same **3-beat structure** works when a pharmaceutical client wants answers fast:

1. **Beat 1 — Headline (10s):** Give the bottom line first.
   - *"To **shed some light on** where the vision test stands..."*
2. **Beat 2 — Cause & Fix (30s):** Explain clearly without sounding defensive.
   - *"We're **ironing out** one edge case in the optical module—it's a software calibration, not a hardware flaw."*
3. **Beat 3 — Action / Boundary (20s):** Propose a concrete next step.
   - *"We recommend **tabling shipping sign-off** for 48 hours until Friday's validation data is in."*

---

## 4. 🤝 Diplomatic Pushback Phrases (Calm & Firm)

Use these when a client pushes for an unrealistic Friday shipment:
- **Acknowledge + Pivot:** *"I completely understand the schedule pressure; **however**, shipping before calibration puts compliance at risk."*
- **Show Value:** *"What we **bring to the table** is zero-defect reliability—a 48-hour buffer now prevents a costly line stop later."*
- **Hold the Line:** *"Let's **table final sign-off** until Friday at noon once the test logs are verified."*
- **Team Coordination:** *"With 16 technicians on one line, we avoid **`herding cats`** by running **staggered shifts** and clear handovers."*
