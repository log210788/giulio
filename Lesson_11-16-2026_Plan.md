## 21–42 min | Core Task: 60-Second Engineering Clarity & Diplomatic Pushback
*The Bridge (1 min): "The same 3-beat 60-second structure (30s / 50s / 20s) works identically when briefing an impatient pharma client during crunch time."*

### The 3-Beat Executive Frame (Show on Screen)
1. **Beat 1 — Headline (10s):** Give the bottom line first (*"To **shed some light on** where the vision test stands..."*).
2. **Beat 2 — Core Cause & Action (30s):** Name the issue without sounding defensive (*"We're **ironing out** one edge case in the optical module—it's a software calibration, not a hardware flaw."*).
3. **Beat 3 — Concrete Ask / Boundary (20s):** Propose the next step (*"We recommend **tabling shipping sign-off** for 48 hours until Friday's validation data is in."*).

### 3 Timed Scenarios (Lewis plays the International Client PM)
- **Scenario 1: The 60-Second Status Update (Timed — No Interruptions)**
  - **Client Prompt:** *"Giulio, give me a straight 60-second update: why did yesterday's optical vision test fail, and how do we know it's not a hardware defect?"*
  - **Giulio's Goal:** Hit all 3 beats in **≤ 60 seconds** using `shed light on` and `iron out`.

- **Scenario 2: Diplomatic Pushback Under Pressure (Live Back-and-Forth, 5 mins)**
  - **Client Pushback Lines (Lewis delivers one at a time):**
    1. *"Look, our launch schedule is tight. Can't we just ship on Friday and patch the software remotely later?"*
    2. *"We already waited for third-party approval—why is there another delay?"*
  - **Target Pushback Frames for Giulio:**
    - *"I completely understand the schedule pressure; **however**, shipping before calibration puts accuracy at risk."*
    - *"To be transparent, **what we bring to the table** is zero-defect compliance—so a 48-hour buffer now prevents a line shutdown later."*
    - *"Let's **table the final sign-off** until Friday at noon."*

- **Scenario 3: Explaining Double Shifts & Team Alignment (5 mins)**
